Leaf a Tip is a creator support platform. Buy a pack of credits once, then send a high five to anyone, any time — no re-entering a card, no repeated charges.

What makes it different

01

Prepaid, not per-charge

Buy a pack once, then send support anywhere, any time — no separate card charge for every single send. Save your card and the next pack is a single tap.

02

Every creator gets a page

Bio, photo, socials, custom links — a real profile, not just a bare payment button.

03

Real payouts

Stripe Connect under the hood. Creators hold their balance and cash out to their own bank whenever they're ready.

04

Creators keep 100%

Nothing is taken out of a high five — the full amount reaches the creator. The platform runs on Low Fives instead: one $1 Low Five added to every $5 of credit you buy.

05

Live balance & history

Every pack, send, and payout tracked in real time — visible to both the sender and the creator.

{five} and then some

Who it's for

Leaf a Tip is built for creators, streamers, and small communities who want a simple way to receive support without asking their fans to think twice.

Buy once, then send to anyone whenever the moment calls for it — no re-entering a card, no second-guessing whether it's worth the hassle for a few dollars.

And for the people running those communities who want their own branded page instead of a generic tip button.
