What makes it different

01

Branded for your client

Each business gets its own themed booking experience — colors, language, and layout that match their brand, not yours.

02

Two booking models

Capacity-based for tours and classes (multiple groups share a slot until full), or appointment-style for consultations and sessions.

03

One platform, many clients

Add new businesses without writing code. Each tenant is fully isolated — data, branding, and bookings never bleed between clients.

04

Real-time availability

Seat counts update instantly as bookings come in. Customers see exactly how many spots remain before they commit.

05

Owner dashboard

Manage services, pricing, capacity, and bookings from a clean admin interface. Built on Firebase — real-time, isolated, no backend to manage.

{five} and then some

Who it's for

FlexAgenda is built for small and mid-size businesses that have outgrown generic booking tools but don't have the budget for custom enterprise software.

Tour operators who need group capacity management. Tax consultants who need calendar-style appointments. Creatives and service businesses who want their booking page to feel like an extension of their brand — not a third-party widget.

And the consultants, agencies, and developers who build and manage those businesses' digital presence.
